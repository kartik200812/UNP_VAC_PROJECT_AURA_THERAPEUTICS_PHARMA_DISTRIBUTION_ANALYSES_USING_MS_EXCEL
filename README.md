# UNP_VAC_PROJECT_AURA_THERAPEUTICS_PHARMA_DISTRIBUTION_ANALYSES_USING_MS_EXCEL

Successfully completed a Data Analytics and Artificial Intelligence project in collaboration with UNP, gaining practical experience in data processing, statistical analysis, and AI-assisted analytical techniques. The project involved working with datasets and performing data preprocessing, including data cleaning, organizing, and formatting. Using Microsoft Excel, I created bar charts and Pivot Tables to summarize and visualize data effectively. I also performed statistical tests such as Z-Test, T-Test, and ANOVA to analyze differences and test hypotheses. Additionally, I applied simple and multiple linear regression to understand relationships between dependent and independent variables. The project also explored the role of Artificial Intelligence in data analytics, including identifying patterns, interpreting data, automating tasks, and generating meaningful insights. This experience enhanced my technical skills, analytical thinking, problem-solving abilities, and understanding of how AI and data analytics contribute to informed, data-driven business decision-making.
